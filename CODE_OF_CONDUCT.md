# Community Conduct

Vidrensic welcomes technical discussion, reproducible research and respectful disagreement.

Participants are expected to:

- focus criticism on evidence, code and methodology rather than people;
- describe uncertainty and limitations accurately;
- avoid publishing confidential case material or personal information;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse or deliberate disruption;
- respect software, dataset and reverse-engineering licenses;
- report security-sensitive findings through `SECURITY.md` rather than public issues.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content that exposes sensitive evidence, contains abuse, or materially disrupts technical collaboration. Repeated or serious violations may result in restricted participation.

For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through an appropriate GitHub channel rather than posting private details publicly.
