Vidrensic may invoke or interoperate with third-party programs and libraries such as FFmpeg/ffprobe and GNU ddrescue. Their licenses remain independent and must be respected. A Vidrensic license grant does not relicense third-party software.

## Forensic use

Vidrensic is under active development and validation. A successful scan, recovery, or validation result does not by itself establish legal admissibility, laboratory accreditation, chain-of-custody compliance, or fitness for a specific investigation.

Operators are responsible for preserving source evidence, recording acquisition and examination procedures, validating outputs, and determining whether the version used is appropriate for the intended purpose.

## Contributions

Unless a separate written agreement states otherwise, contributions submitted to this repository must be submitted by someone who has the right to license those contributions under the repository's contribution and licensing terms.

Contributors must not submit customer evidence, active-case CCTV, confidential material, encryption keys, proprietary recorder dumps, or third-party content they are not authorized to redistribute.
